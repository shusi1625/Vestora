# Competitive Framing

## Problem

Vestora can look too similar to existing token streaming primitives if it is described only as ERC-20 escrow plus time-based claim plus ERC-721 ownership.

## Positioning

Frame Vestora as:

> a gas-aware dApp architecture for trading, disclosing risk, and evaluating transferable on-chain future receivable NFTs.

## What Not To Claim

Do not claim Vestora is a complete legal receivables or RWA platform. The project uses ERC-20 based on-chain payment streams as an experimental model for future receivable trading.

Do not overstate production readiness or enterprise infrastructure. AWS is used for service-like operational evidence and metrics.

## Differentiation Axes

1. Economic meaning of stream-based future receivable NFTs
2. Gas-aware on-chain/off-chain architecture
3. Performance-centered UI/UX for fast value judgment

## Sablier Relationship

Sablier is a baseline and comparison point, not something to ignore. Vestora should differentiate through marketplace valuation, risk disclosure, buyer protection, backend indexing, off-chain analytics, and operational metrics.

## Report Claim Rule

Every comparison claim should be linked to one of:

- implemented feature
- measured performance result
- gas table
- UI screenshot or scenario
- architecture diagram or documented boundary
- cited prior-service analysis in the final report
